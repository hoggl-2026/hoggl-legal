You have the right to delete your Hoggl account and request the removal of your personal data at any time. This page explains how to do that, what data is deleted, and what we are required to retain.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request that your Hoggl account and associated personal data be deleted by contacting us directly:

  • Email: info@the4thprinciple.com
  • Please use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Hoggl account

We will acknowledge your request within 5 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

2. What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we will remove the following personal data:

  • Your name, email address, and profile information
  • Your delivery address and postcode
  • Your listings and associated photographs
  • Your messages with other users
  • Your device and app usage data

Your Stripe seller account, if you have one, will also be disconnected. Please refer to Stripe's privacy policy for information on how they handle your payment and identity data.

3. What Data We Are Required to Retain

In certain circumstances, we are required by law to retain some data even after your account has been deleted:

  • Transaction records — records of completed transactions may be retained for up to 7 years in line with UK financial record-keeping and tax requirements, including our obligations under the DAC7 regulations
  • Fraud prevention — where we have a legitimate interest in retaining data to prevent fraud or misuse of the platform
  • Legal disputes — where data is relevant to an ongoing or reasonably anticipated legal claim

Any data retained after account deletion will be held securely and used only for the purposes set out above.

4. Requesting Deletion of Data Without Closing Your Account

If you would like to request deletion of specific data without closing your account — for example, removing a particular listing or message — please contact us at info@the4thprinciple.com with details of what you would like removed. We will consider all such requests in line with our obligations under UK GDPR.

5. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Your right to erasure is one of several data rights you hold under UK GDPR. For a full explanation of your rights, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
